Practicum 2 Lesson Plan 2

یوٹیوب پر آڈیو کے لیے کلک کریں

April 21, 2022
Grade 5th Time 30 Minutes
Subject Mathematics Class strength 25
Chapter 3 Fraction (کسور) Topic Addition of Fraction

1. General Objectives

Students will be able to:

  • develop mathematical curiosity when solving problems.
  • become confident in using mathematics to analyse and solve problems both in school and in real-life situations.
  • develop the knowledge, skills, and attitudes necessary to pursue further studies in mathematics.

2. Specific Objectives (Students Learning Outcomes)

Students will be able to:

  • Identify fractions having the same and different denominators.
  • Add and subtract fractions with the same and different denominators.
  • Solve questions given on page number 37 i.e. exercise 3.1.

3. Materials & Resources

Blackboard, different colour chalks, textbook, different charts to represent fractions.

4. Introduction (5 Minutes)

The teacher will greet the students and before going to the topic s/he will inquire about their daily chores and activities. Furthermore, the teacher will ask questions from the students to recall what they had studied about fractions in their last class. This activity will help them to connect their today's lesson with the previous one. A few questions the teacher will ask the students while greeting are as follows:

  1. How are you doing these days?
  2. How are you spending your Ramadan?
  3. Do you remember the fraction we studied in the last class?
  4. Can any of you tell us what is a fraction?

5. Lesson (15 Minutes)

After recalling and checking the homework the teacher will start teaching about adding fractions of the same denominators. In the last class, we had already built their understanding and concept of fractions having the same denominators. As the students understand and can solve questions related to the same denominators, we will move to fractions having different denominators.

6. Discussion and Questions (8 Minutes)

As the teacher explains the concept of addition and subtraction, now the students will be asked about their confusion. This time practice method will be used to explain the concepts. We have co-teachers present. They will assist students even when needed individually to solve questions of exercise 3.1 on page 37.

7. Assessment

The teacher will assess the students and reflect upon their teaching method. The teacher with the assistance of a co-teacher will observe the students solving questions that will be an assessment.

8. Homework (2 minutes)

The students will be assigned to solve part exercise 3.1. And they shall be instructed to mark the question they weren't able to solve.

Note: Co-teacher N delivered the lesson.

Feedback to Co-teacher N

The overall experience of today's co-teaching was good, as you managed the class exceptionally well.

Some of the positives are:

  • The students were responding when you were asking different questions at the start and at the end of the lesson. There was little or no disturbance during the lesson, as there were appropriate moments in class, and your beady eye was on the balcony, watching every student.
  • You tried to start the lesson where we left off last time, despite a gap of more than three weeks, by asking questions — very important in teaching, as the connection is vital to recall prior learning.
  • The other good thing was the checking of homework. All the students who were present last time at least tried to complete it, and most of them succeeded.
  • The last thing I like is that you kept the promise of the previous lesson, where Ms Humera had promised a reward to the students, by giving them some candies. When we keep our promises, people trust us — in this case, the student will trust the teacher, which is a must-have between the two.

And certainly, there were a few things that could have been improved:

  • Your voice was on the low side. You have to practise speaking a little louder. Yes, the number of students in this particular class was fewer, but what if there is a huge classroom with more than 30 students?
  • The second thing which needs some improvement is the content knowledge. You prepared the lesson well, but you could put more effort into further strengthening your concepts about the topic "fractions." One thing you can do is give a demo in front of co-teachers or ask for clarifications from someone you like, so the loopholes in the lecture can be identified on time.
  • Another minor thing that needs some attention is the correct use of mathematical language and symbols — like 1/2 and 1/4 being called half and a quarter, respectively, and using "=" instead of "//" for equality. The rationale is to enhance mathematical vocabulary; the wrong use of symbols by teachers would lead to the same by students, as they imitate whatever a teacher tells or presents to the class.

I hope you take this feedback constructively and that it helps you improve in the future.

Feedback to Co-teacher N by Co-teacher H

The student-teacher performed really well. She managed the class and, despite its size and crowding, interacted with every student. She connected the previous lesson with the day's lesson.

Some areas of improvement:

  • Audibility: She wasn't audible to everyone, and she should ensure her words flow in a rhythmic pattern so students enjoy the lesson. While teaching, she should also create some form of drama.
  • Mathematical terms: the student-teacher should use mathematical terms, particularly in Urdu, as it is the medium of communication and their course is taught in Urdu.
